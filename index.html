WELCOME TO YOUR TABLE

A place to play things out.

Import decks for four seats, or load the offline practice table. Only the controlled player's hand is visible; the other hands show card backs. Switching either seat selector changes both the controlled seat and pinned hand. Pan across the table to visit the other seats. The highlighted seat receives toolbar actions; the gold dot marks the active turn.

Space + drag / middle-drag
Pan the view. You can also drag empty felt.
Scroll / + / −
Zoom at the pointer. Shift-scroll pans or scrolls a hand.
1–4 / 0
Jump to a seat / see the whole table. The overview map also navigates.
Pinned hand
Your hand stays at the bottom while you pan. Hover to enlarge cards; drag to play or return a card. Choose its player in the hand selector.
Drag a card
Move between zones, other seats, or the stack. Arrange your battlefield freely.
Left Alt + hover
Hold Left Alt over a card for its artwork, rules and controls. Move into the controls to use them; release Alt and move away to dismiss.
Hover a card
The card under your cursor is selected for shortcuts. Move away to clear selection. Hold Left Alt to inspect or use its controls.
Double-click / right-click / T
Activate a battlefield source, choosing a color or paid ability when available. Shift+T taps without activating.
F
Turn the hovered card face down or face up. Commanders, graveyard, exile, and opponents' hands cannot be flipped.
Ctrl+Z / ⌘Z
Undo a table action.
Click a pile
Browse library, graveyard, or exile. Drag a pile to take its top card: the next card in the library, or the newest card in graveyard/exile.

Next step and End turn move the turn marker. Enable Auto untap / draw for those two automatic actions. Use the mana tray to track floating mana. Bright spells can be afforded; click to pay and cast. Responses: auto skips unavailable responses; Responses: all gives every seat the same window for privacy. Other card effects and deck legality remain manual.
