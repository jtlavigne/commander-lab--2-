# Launch landscapes

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-14 for this project. Both original PNG assets are 2172 × 724 (3:1). No external image service is needed at runtime.

- `emerald-sanctuary.png`: ancient forest valley and citadel at dawn.
- `moonlit-archipelago.png`: floating islands and arcane observatories at night.

The launch screen randomly selects one at startup, slowly pans horizontally, and advances to the other landscape every two minutes while the menu is visible. Settings → Animate menu backgrounds pauses motion and rotation. The operating system's reduced-motion preference also disables panning.
